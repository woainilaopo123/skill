# Core Concepts

## Purpose

Use this reference when the task is to explain settlement terminology, break down amounts, or derive settlement formulas from business language.

## Stable Term Mapping

Use the following ASCII aliases in the skill text:

- `zong-feiyong`: total medical expense
- `yibao-fanwei-zonge`: medical-insurance settlement-scope amount
- `fei-yibao-zifei`: non-insurance-scope personal self-pay
- `fenlei-zifu`: category self-burden
- `jiaoyi-zonge`: transaction expense total
- `yibao-baoxiao`: insurance reimbursement or fund payment
- `geren-zifu`: personal burden after reimbursement
- `geren-zifu-total`: personal self-pay in insurance path
- `geren-zifei`: personal self-pay outside insurance path
- `geren-xianjin-zhifu`: actual out-of-pocket amount

## Core Definitions

### zong-feiyong

The total amount generated by the visit.

At a high level:

```text
zong-feiyong = jijin-zhifu-zonge + geren-xianjin-zhifu + yiyuan-fudan + qita-zhichu
```

A useful simplified identity is:

```text
zong-feiyong = yibao-baoxiao + geren-xianjin-zhifu
```

### yibao-fanwei-zonge

The total amount inside insurance policy scope. This includes `fenlei-zifu`.

### fei-yibao-zifei

Costs outside policy scope, such as:
- full self-pay drugs or items
- over-limit self-pay caused by capped insurance prices

Common summary:

```text
geren-zifei = quan-zifei + chao-xiane-zifei
```

### fenlei-zifu

The patient-first burden within insurance-covered items, often seen in category-paid items such as some class-B drugs.

This amount remains inside insurance scope, but does not stay inside the effective reimbursement base.

### jiaoyi-zonge

The effective expense base entering settlement after category self-burden is removed.

```text
jiaoyi-zonge = yibao-fanwei-zonge - fenlei-zifu
```

### yibao-baoxiao

The fund-paid portion. Depending on context, it may correspond to:
- insurance reimbursement amount
- fund payment amount
- center-paid amount

### geren-zifu

The patient-borne amount remaining inside settlement scope after reimbursement.

```text
jiaoyi-zonge = yibao-baoxiao + geren-zifu
```

### geren-zifu-total

The self-pay amount within the insurance path.

```text
geren-zifu-total = geren-zifu + fenlei-zifu
```

### geren-zifei

The self-pay amount outside the insurance path.

```text
geren-zifei = quan-zifei + chao-xiane-zifei
```

### geren-xianjin-zhifu

The amount the patient actually bears.

```text
geren-xianjin-zhifu = geren-zifu-total + geren-zifei
```

Expanded:

```text
geren-xianjin-zhifu = geren-zifu + fenlei-zifu + quan-zifei + chao-xiane-zifei
```

## General Formula Set

Use these first unless the task requires a platform-specific formula set.

```text
zong-feiyong = yibao-fanwei-zonge + fei-yibao-zifei
jiaoyi-zonge = yibao-fanwei-zonge - fenlei-zifu
jiaoyi-zonge = yibao-baoxiao + geren-zifu
geren-zifu-total = geren-zifu + fenlei-zifu
geren-zifei = quan-zifei + chao-xiane-zifei
geren-xianjin-zhifu = geren-zifu-total + geren-zifei
zong-feiyong = yibao-baoxiao + geren-xianjin-zhifu
```

## Relationship Tree

```text
zong-feiyong
├── yibao-fanwei-zonge
│   ├── jiaoyi-zonge
│   │   ├── yibao-baoxiao
│   │   └── geren-zifu
│   └── fenlei-zifu
└── fei-yibao-zifei
    ├── quan-zifei
    └── chao-xiane-zifei
```

## Explanation Notes

- `geren-zifei` is outside insurance scope and usually does not enter the settlement base.
- `fenlei-zifu` is inside insurance scope but deducted before reimbursement-base calculation.
- `jiaoyi-zonge` is usually the most useful amount for "what really enters settlement".
- In business conversations, `geren-zifu-total` and `geren-xianjin-zhifu` are often mixed. Clarify whether the speaker means insurance-path self-pay or actual out-of-pocket payment.
